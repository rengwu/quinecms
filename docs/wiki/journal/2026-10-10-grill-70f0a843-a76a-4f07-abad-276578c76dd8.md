## Question 5

```text
Round: Quine rebuild — scope and CMS trial
Question: How much control should the client have through the CMS?
Description (Markdown source):
Likely edits include headings, service descriptions, project titles and images, links, navigation labels and contact details. Styling controls such as colors, spacing and animation parameters are a separate level of flexibility.
Recommendation: Make ordinary editorial content and section ordering editable. Keep the distinctive design and animation implementation in source unless a specific control helps the owner.
Options: Editorial content and section ordering / Editorial content plus useful visual settings / Only a small set of frequently changed fields
Selected: Editorial content and section ordering
```
