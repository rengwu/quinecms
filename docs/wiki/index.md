# QuineCMS

QuineCMS is a Git-backed static CMS with a visual admin portal. Sites own their
content, configuration, assets and original extensions. The engine and optional
design libraries are pinned dependencies.

- [Feature map](features/index.md): included editor, public-site and developer
  capabilities, grouped with concrete subfeatures.
- [Authoring guides](guides/index.md): building, maintaining and upgrading sites.
- [Trials](trials/index.md): recreations and pilots, the capabilities they exercise,
  and their recorded findings.
- [Verification](verification/index.md): setup, check recipes and dated results.
- [Decisions](decisions/index.md): accepted product choices and their basis.
- [Open questions](discussions/open-questions.md): active topics and settled scope.
