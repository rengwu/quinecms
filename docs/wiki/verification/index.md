# Verification

Verification describes how a capability or trial was checked. A result applies
to its recorded build and scope; it is not a permanent feature status.

- [Setup, fixtures and isolation](setup.md)
- [Feature check recipes and evidence](features/index.md)
- [Trials](../trials/index.md): recreation and pilot scope linked to their results
- [Feature map](../features/index.md): current behavior contracts

New or reorganized recipes are not automatically executed checks. Dated run
records retain their observations and limits, including mocked versus live
providers and maintainer-reported evidence.
